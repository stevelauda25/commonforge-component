POD has two shadow groups:

- **Drop shadows** — the elevation ramp `shadow-foundation-*`, matched 1:1
  to Figma's `shadows/shadow-*` tokens.
- **Brand glow** — `shadow-glow-accent-*` and `shadow-glow-danger-*` baked
  into the primary/error button states. Sacred — never modified by sync.

## Brand glow (sacred)

Inset highlights baked into Primary/Error buttons. **Never** modified by
sync — they define POD's brand identity.

```tsx
  Primary

```

## Source of truth

Values live in [`packages/tokens/src/theme.css`](https://github.com/) under
`/* ── Shadows ── */` and `/* ── Glow effects ── */`. Exposed via
[`packages/tokens/src/tailwind-preset.ts`](https://github.com/) `boxShadow`.
This page reads from the preset directly — adding a new shadow there shows
up here automatically.
