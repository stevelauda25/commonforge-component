POD has two shadow groups:

- **Drop shadows** — the elevation ramp `shadow-foundation-*`, matched 1:1
  to Figma's `shadows/shadow-*` tokens. Note: the Figma scale is intentionally
  subtle (alpha 0.08 / 0.04) — `xs` and `s` look similar; `2xl`/`3xl`
  extend far beyond the swatch.
- **Brand glow** — `shadow-glow-accent-*` and `shadow-glow-danger-*` baked
  into the primary/error button states. Sacred — never modified by sync.

## Brand glow (sacred)
